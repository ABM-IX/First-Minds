### Part 2: Expanded View (Modal / Drawer / Detail View)

*Use this when a visitor clicks the card to see the full project elaboration.*

#### **Overview**
> In emerging cities across Southern Africa, over 85% of motorized daily trips rely on decentralized paratransit—minibus combis, special cabs, and intercity coaches. Due to a lack of live scheduling and telematics, commuters face unpredictable 20–45 minute wait times, while operators waste fuel racing and bunching along identical corridors.
> 
> **SmartTransit** bridges this critical gap. Built by **FastMind**, it provides an end-to-end Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) that synchronizes passengers, drivers, and municipal transit regulators into one unified, real-time mobility network.

---

#### **Key Capabilities & Innovations**

1. **Algorithmic Anti-Bunching Headway Control**
   * Employs mathematical headway regularization to broadcast real-time in-cab pacing advisories (`MAINTAIN PACE`, `SLOW DOWN`, `HOLD`) directly to combi drivers over low-latency WebSockets, preventing vehicle platooning and passenger starvation intervals.

2. **Speed-Floored Real-Time ETAs**
   * Replaces brittle linear tracking with a speed-floored arrival estimator that eliminates false infinite ETAs during rank dwell times and traffic bottlenecks.

3. **Multi-Modal Graph Journey Planner**
   * Integrates fixed-route combis, point-to-point charter taxis ($k$-NN spatial dispatch), and intercity bus lines into a single Dijkstra graph solver that coordinates transfers, schedules, and gazetted fares in one search.

4. **Regulatory Dispatch Command Center**
   * A desktop administrative console (React + Leaflet) giving municipal authorities (such as DRTS) live fleet visualization, route density heatmaps, and automated statutory audit reporting.

---

#### **Target Value & Impact**
* **For Commuters:** Transparent live vehicle tracking, upfront statutory fares, and predictable departure times.
* **For Drivers & Operators:** Fuel savings, even passenger distribution, and automated on-demand hail dispatch.
* **For Municipal Regulators:** First-ever digitized transit telemetry, route capacity metrics, and safety enforcement data.

---

#### **System Architecture & Tech Stack**
* **Mobile Applications:** Flutter (Dart) — High-performance cross-platform driver and commuter apps.
* **Backend & Telematics Engine:** Python 3.11, FastAPI, Python-SocketIO (1Hz real-time telemetry streaming).
* **Spatial & Database Layer:** PostgreSQL 15 with PostGIS spatial extensions, Supabase Realtime pub/sub.
* **Operations Command Center:** React 18, Vite, Leaflet GIS mapping.
